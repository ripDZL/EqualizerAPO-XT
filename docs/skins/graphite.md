# Graphite Clarity

Graphite is a charcoal high-readability instrument panel. It replaces pure
black with layered dark-grey surfaces and uses square wire plates, broad signal
rails, and dense spacing so it is a distinct working language from Clarity.

## Contract

- Dark mode is charcoal: `#191C20` is the outer field, never pure black.
- Primary labels and values exceed 15:1 contrast on cards; secondary labels
  exceed 9:1 on normal, hover, and selected surfaces.
- Knobs, keyboard focus, selected choices, hover, press, and disabled states
  use geometry or fill as well as colour.
- Modern cards and Legacy Rows use the shared high-visibility painters,
  including the bipolar Preamp `AudioKnob`.
- The required readability Legacy gallery captures both Clarity and Graphite
  in dark/light active and disabled states.

## Form language

Graphite uses a small family of charcoal planes—field, surface, card, and
hover—rather than a black void. It has square card and graph corners, a broad
left signal rail, wireframe channel badges, 48px rows, and gradient scope bars.
White text, firm grey boundaries, and a clean blue action colour make controls
readable without neon decoration.

## Do not

- Flatten the dark palette into black; Clarity owns the black-and-white mode.
- Reduce borders or knob travel to make it look softer.
- Use grey-on-grey text or colour alone to express state.
